# **App Name**: PepsiConnect

## Core Features:

- Shop-wise Order Creation: Enable sales reps to create and submit orders directly from the app for specific shops.
- Product Catalog: Maintain a comprehensive and up-to-date catalog of all Pepsi products with details (SKUs, prices, sizes, offers).
- Order Status Tracking: Provide real-time tracking of orders through different stages: Pending, Confirmed, Dispatched, Delivered.
- Current Stock Dashboard: Display real-time stock levels at warehouses or delivery vans to help manage inventory effectively.
- Sales Dashboard: Generate daily, weekly, and monthly sales summaries for at-a-glance performance assessment.
- AI Sales Forecast: Tool to predict product demand to reduce spoilage.
- Admin Web Dashboard: Admin panel (web dashboard) that gives full visibility over orders, sales data, shop information, approve orders, assign delivery routes to staff, and more.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Vibrant Pepsi Blue (#4386F9) to reflect the brand identity. The choice of the saturated blue hue aligns directly with the pepsi brand's visual presence and conveys brand awareness in users of the app. We can see a need to stay grounded with pepsi, a company and brand loved all over the world!
- Background color: Light blue (#E6F0FF), a lighter tint of the primary blue for a clean and modern look.
- Accent color: A bright yellow (#FFD700) is chosen because yellow can also be an 'attention getter' on its own
- Font pairing: 'Poppins' (sans-serif) for headings and 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for body text, offering a balance of modernity and readability.
- Use clean, modern icons, mostly monochromatic but highlighted with the accent color to guide users through the app.
- Implement a clean, intuitive layout with clear sections for easy navigation, optimized for quick order placement and data review.
- Incorporate subtle animations to provide feedback and enhance the user experience, such as loading animations and transitions between screens.
